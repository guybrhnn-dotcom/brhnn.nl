Source: Google Fonts (github.com/google/fonts). Playfair Display, Source Serif 4 and Libre Franklin are
variable fonts; the files here are static instances made with fontTools (varLib.instancer):
  Krantkop-Black          Playfair Display wght=900, renamed
  Krantkop-Italic         Playfair Display Italic wght=400, renamed
  SourceSerif4-Regular    wght=400 opsz=16  SourceSerif4-Italic     wght=400 opsz=16
  LibreFranklin-SemiBold  wght=600          LibreFranklin-Bold      wght=700
Playfair Display has the Reserved Font Name "Playfair Display", so a modified version may not use
that name: the instances are called "Krantkop". Source Serif 4 and Libre Franklin have no reserved name.
UnifrakturMaguntia-Book is the original, unmodified file.
Under the OFL the fonts may be bundled with the app; they may not be sold on their own.
